We're a group of parents, staff, faculty, and concerned community members working to keep Fort Hill open. We'd love your support as we work to reverse this decision and seek clarity and stability for families and the community.

The best way to get involved is to join the fight: sign up and tell us how you'd like to help. For anything else, email us.
